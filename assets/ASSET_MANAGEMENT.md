# FinMang OS Asset Management

## Purpose

This document defines how financial assets are organized and tracked.

---

## Asset Types

- Cash
- Bank Account
- Mobile Wallet
- Gold
- Investment
- Cryptocurrency
- Emergency Fund

---

## Asset Information

Each asset contains:

- Name
- Category
- Current Balance
- Currency
- Institution
- Status
- Last Updated

---

## Planned Features

- Net Worth Calculation
- Asset Growth Tracking
- Historical Balance
- Monthly Snapshot
- Multi-currency Support
